description=Blood syncing is now done on the server instead of in the client through OnClothingUpdated. This should fix every error related to blood sync as well as some mod incompatibilities.
description=Every few ticks the server increments through the player list to check if each player's face/muscle needs its blood updating.
description=The delay between each check adjusts based on the amount of players in order to reduce strain on the server.
description=The max delay between checks is currently set at 200 ticks so with 2 players it will check a player every 100 ticks and with 100 players it would check a player every 2 ticks.
description=This effectively means that there is a 200 tick delay for a player's blood to be updated which shouldn't be too noticeable in gameplay. This tick delay also applies to singleplayer.
description=I can lower the delay if people think it's too noticeable.
description=
description=There are still some minor syncing issues in mp with blood but it isn't very noticeable and it will go back to normal again anyway whenever the player changes their clothing.

description=[h1]Faces and Details[/h1]
description=    [b]Select from different faces and add additional detail textures to your character[/b]
description=
description=	Faces work on all skintones and new options can be added by other mods.
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[quote]
description=[h1]Muscles[/h1]
description=    [b]Characters will have visible muscles at high strength levels[/b]
description=
description=	Can be toggled globally or set to player preference in sandbox settings.
description=
description=    [b]MUSCLE TEXTURES DO NOT WORK WITH UNSUPPORTED TEXTURE MODS LIKE SIMPLE CHARACTER RETEXTURE.
description=	TURN OFF MUSCLES IN SANDBOX IF YOU ARE USING ONE THAT DOESN'T SUPPORT THIS MOD[/b]
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[quote]
description=[h1]Body Hair[/h1]
description=    [b]Head stubble, beard stubble, and body hair (formerly chest hair) will grow in-game and can be shaved[/b]
description=
description=	[b]The button to shave your stubble and body hair is in the character info panel.[/b]
description=
description=	Vanilla chest hair has been completely replaced with a new system that works on both body types.
description=
description=	Can be toggled globally or set to player preference in sandbox settings.
description=
description=
description=    [b]THIS MOD DOES NOT AFFECT HAIR OR BEARD GROWTH OUTSIDE OF SHAVING HEAD/BEARD STUBBLE[/b]
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[quote]
description=[h1]Included with this mod[/h1]
description=[list]
description=    [*][b]Base mod[/b] - Framework, doesn't add any customisation options
description=
description=    [*][b]Vanilla Faces Pack[/b] - Adds 4 male and 4 female faces. Only supports the vanilla body textures [b]DO NOT USE THIS WITH RETEXTURE MODS[/b]
description=
description=    [*][b]Details Pack[/b] - Adds freckles, moles, scars, burns, and missing eyes.
description=	Missing eyes also affect the players vision.
description=
description=    [*][b]Details Pack HD Textures[/b] - 512x512 textures for the Details Pack.
description=[b]DO NOT USE THE HD TEXTURE PACK with vanilla textures or any retextures that are 256x256 or it will cause seams.[/b]
description=[/list]
description=[/quote]
