description=
description=    [b]Select from different faces and add additional detail textures to your character[/b]

description=
description=[h1]Visible Muscles[/h1]
description=
description=    [b]Characters will have visible muscles at high strength levels[/b]
description=	
description=	Muscle intensity increases at level 6 and level 9 strength (Stout and Strong traits in character creation).
description=	
description=	Can be toggled globally or set to player preference in sandbox settings.
description=	
description=    [b]MUSCLE TEXTURES DO NOT WORK WITH UNSUPPORTED TEXTURE MODS LIKE SIMPLE CHARACTER RETEXTURE. 
description=	TURN OFF MUSCLES IN SANDBOX IF YOU ARE USING ONE THAT DOESN'T SUPPORT THIS MOD[/b]
description=
description=
description=[hr]
description=
description=[h1]Body Hair[/h1]
description=
description=    [b]Head stubble, beard stubble, and body hair (formerly chest hair) will grow in-game and can be shaved[/b]
description=	
description=	[b]The button to shave your stubble and body hair is in the character info panel.[/b]
description=	
description=	Vanilla chest hair has been completely replaced with a new system that works on both body types.
description=	
description=	Can be toggled globally or set to player preference in sandbox settings.
description=	
description=	
description=    [b]THIS MOD DOES NOT AFFECT HAIR OR BEARD GROWTH OUTSIDE OF SHAVING HEAD/BEARD STUBBLE[/b]
description=
description=
description=[hr]
description=

description=Joining a game with a character from before the mod was added will open the character customisation window so that you can pick faces and details.

description=[h1]Technical Details[/h1]
description=
description=See the readme and Example.lua to learn how you can make addons for the mod.
description=
description=The body hair textures in the base mod will cut off at the underwear which won't look right when using a nude mod.
description=[b]I've included nude versions of the body hair textures in the files for other modders to use if they want to make their mod compatible.[/b]
description=
description=
description=The blood/dirt on the face and muscles are occasionally synced to the body's blood/dirt levels so there will always be a slight delay which I've never noticed during gameplay.
description=
description=To reduce strain on the server, every few ticks the server will try to sync the blood of a single player until it goes through the list of active players. 
description=The tick delay adjusts based on the number of players and currently it will take around 200 ticks to finish checking every player once.
description=This means that in singleplayer it will do a check every 200 ticks but in a 100 player server it would be doing a check every 2 ticks. 
description=Basically there is always a roughly 2 second delay for blood on a player's face or muscle to become accurate.
description=
